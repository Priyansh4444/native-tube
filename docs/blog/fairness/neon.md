## Overall Fairness Verdict

**UNFAIR AS WRITTEN (FIXABLE MOSTLY BY WORDING/LABEL EDITS, WITH 3 TARGETED RE-MEASUREMENTS NEEDED FOR STRICT APPLES-TO-APPLES RANKINGS).**

The article is much more disciplined than a typical framework shootout: it already separates AC vs. battery, laptop vs. Fly, fixture replay vs. live YouTube, and ad time vs. video load time, and its two headline architectural findings—**~4.3–4.7× playback CPU / ~19× instruction collapse from the Wayland GPU subsurface on real silicon**, and **the reversal into a loss under CPU-emulated EGL (`llvmpipe`) on Fly**—are genuinely fair and rock-solid.

However, **the cross-framework rankings and several specific comparisons fail a strict fairness bar** for concrete, code-verified reasons that are currently undisclosed or under-caveated:

1. **The apps do not do the same work.** On Watch open, GPUI and Rune eagerly fetch and parse comments (`comments_loaded`), while iced skips comments entirely—flattering iced's Watch latency, warm-switch instructions, and Watch/tour memory. Conversely, autopilot `Command::Scroll` forces 27 extra `view()` rebuilds in iced that real wheel input skips, while Rune starts the laptop wheel phase with `9.06 MB` of thumbnails already cached (`>2×` iced/GPUI's `3.95 MB`) and draws `0` UI frames during 10 s GPU playback (vs. `10` in iced and `11` in GPUI). iced also disables antialiasing (`.antialiasing(false)`), undisclosed.
2. **Prewarm policies are asymmetric (and `HEAD` has drifted from `NOW`).** At `NOW` (`1b6b7c6`), iced and GPUI prewarm both `libmpv` and the Wayland EGL surface before the first click, while Rune prewarms only `libmpv`. That penalizes Rune in `open_ms` (where iced is declared winner) and flatters Rune in Warm Home PSS (where `fly-bench.md:13` inaccurately says all default runs attached an EGL surface). Commit `657b90d` later removed Rune's idle `libmpv` prewarm altogether.
3. **Probe hooks measure different pipeline stages.** `HOME_LOADED` and `PLAYBACK_STARTED` fire pre-GPU-submit in iced and GPUI, but post-encode/present in Rune.
4. **Statistical overclaiming and omitted counter-metrics.** "iced won every latency metric" fails spread checks (`open_ms` overlaps GPUI; Rune's 30.1 ms switch beat every iced run; iced hit ~1,000 ms cold-start outliers on run 1 of both `metrics-now` and `counters/home`). The laptop live-network sentence (`x-article.md:74`) hides `n = 2` (contradicting §9's `n = 3` claim), hides Helium's 2.0–6.3 s spread, mixes all-6 `nt-core` PSS (`~141 MiB`) against cold-only Helium PSS (`978 MiB`), and omits `nt-core`'s `0.612`-core CPU right next to Helium's `0.638` cores. §5 omits perf-gate navigation memory retention (`warmed_retained_pss_mib`, `late_growth_pss_mib`), where GPUI beats iced.
