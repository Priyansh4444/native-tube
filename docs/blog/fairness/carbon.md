## 1. Overall Fairness Verdict

**UNFAIR AS WRITTEN (FIXABLE MOSTLY BY WORDING AND VIDEO LABELS; TWO CLAIMS REQUIRE RE-MEASUREMENT IF KEPT AS HEADLINES).**

The core engineering result—routing video onto a Wayland GPU subsurface cuts laptop playback CPU ~4.3–4.7× and user-space instructions ~19×, while flipping into a ~2.9-core CPU loss under `llvmpipe` on a GPU-less VM—is solid, reproducible, and fair across all three toolkits. Normalized CPU math (`cores_busy`, `machine_percent`, unscaled hybrid-PMU instruction sums) is also sound.

However, **seven concrete fairness defects** currently tilt rankings toward **iced** (and native apps over YouTube web) beyond what the controlled data supports:

1. **Asymmetric prewarm + stale `NOW` vs. `HEAD` policy:** At the measured `NOW` snapshot (`1b6b7c6`), iced and GPUI prewarm both `libmpv` and the EGL/Wayland GPU surface before `open_video`, while Rune only prewarmed `libmpv` and paid synchronous EGL attach inside `open_video`. Under symmetric forced-software policy (`libmpv` only, no EGL surface), ~80–90% of Rune's `open_ms` gap disappears (`294 / 300 / 317 ms` on Fly; `306 / 323 / 347 ms` on laptop). Furthermore, later commit `657b90d` (`HEAD~2`) **removed Rune's idle `libmpv` prewarm and `NT_NO_PREWARM` hook altogether**, so `NOW` no longer reflects shipped `HEAD` for Rune.
2. **Unequal work per scenario across apps:**
   - **Watch comments:** On every `open_video` at scroll offset 0, GPUI and Rune eagerly fetch, parse, and measure Page 1 of comments (`"comments_loaded"` in `report.json`), while iced loads zero comments until scrolled.
   - **Laptop Rune double thumbnail decode:** Because `apps/rune/src/view.rs:1200,1257` plans and requests Home thumbnails before `on_layout` updates viewport geometry/scale, Rune decoded **8.10–9.06 MiB** of `nt-core` thumbnails at Home on the laptop vs. **3.95 MiB** for iced and GPUI (on single-output Fly, Rune decoded **4.93 MiB**). That inflates Rune's laptop memory and shifts thumbnail-decode work out of Rune's counted `wheel` window (`+3.11 MiB` new cache bytes during laptop wheel vs. `+4.34 MiB` iced, `+5.05 MiB` GPUI).
   - **Autopilot scroll tax on iced:** Each of the 27 `Command::Scroll` messages routes through `App::update` in iced (`apps/iced/src/app.rs:3507`), forcing 27 full `view` rebuilds that real wheel input skips, whereas Rune's `drain_autopilot` (`apps/rune/src/app.rs:1904`) skips view commits.
3. **Statistical over-claiming (`n = 3` overlaps called wins):** `"iced won every latency metric"` (`docs/blog/x-article.md:41`) claims wins where median gaps are smaller than 3-run spreads: laptop `open_ms` (`94.7 vs. 117.5 ms`, Δ `22.8 ms` < GPUI spread `33.4 ms`; GPUI run 1 `91.0 ms` beat 2 of 3 iced runs), laptop `warm_ms` (`35.5 vs. 50.5 ms`, Δ `15.0 ms` < Rune spread `23.9 ms`; Rune run 3 `30.1 ms` was fastest overall), and laptop `window_ms`/`home_ms` where iced run 1 hit **`980.5 / 1,003.5 ms`** (`793–797 ms` spread). System-wide GPU wheel cycles (`x-article.md:95`) are also used to claim a ~2× GPUI advantage despite `normalized.md:76` ruling them `"unsuitable for a clean frontend comparison"`.
4. **Omitted Fly memory rankings where Rune wins:** Section 5 highlights iced winning every laptop memory row and calls Rune `"heaviest in GPU playback (274 MiB) and tour peak (337 MiB)"`, then anonymizes Fly native peaks as `422–485 MiB` (`x-article.md:126`)—hiding that **`422 MiB` is Rune (leanest on Fly, beating iced `427 MiB` and GPUI `485 MiB`)**, that Rune had the lowest Fly Warm Home PSS (`220 MiB`), and omitting `summary.json`'s `software_peak_pss_mib` where Rune is also leanest (`368 vs. 376 vs. 430 MiB`).
5. **Unfair live-network baseline (`nt-core` vs. Helium, `x-article.md:74`):** Compares headless, windowless `nt-core` (`451 ms`, `129–141 MiB`, no Wayland window or GPU init) against full-windowed Helium (`4,170 ms`, `978 MiB`) from an **undisclosed `n = 2`** run (`bench/results/playback.json:8,217,261`) where Helium's two cold runs were **`2,025 ms` and `6,316 ms`**, while omitting the **windowed GPU `mpvdirect` row** (`1,541 ms`, `373 MiB`) from the exact same file.
6. **Video overlays & end cards (`SideBySideFly.mp4`, `FirstFrame.mp4`, `Memory.mp4`):** `tools/viz/tours.py:212-237` computes `"+X ms behind"` badges from **cumulative tour elapsed time**, carrying earlier step waits and YouTube's live pre-roll ad into `Related` and `Back 1/2`, and scores live-network YouTube against local-fixture native apps. `FirstFrame` and `Memory` hero end cards (`tools/viz/scenes.py:41,43,261,263`) spotlight YouTube's cold live page load/PSS without on-screen workload caveats and mix Laptop vs. Fly badges on the same card.
7. **Unlabelled within-laptop battery vs. AC CPU columns:** `normalized.py:109,131` takes laptop playback `cores_busy` from battery (`power` profile) runs and laptop scroll `cores_busy` from AC (`balance_performance`) runs; `x-article.md` places both in §2/§4/§8/§9 without per-table power-regime labels despite warning at `line 281` never to compare raw numbers across them.
