# NativeTube Framework Take (`carbon`): iced vs. GPUI vs. Rune

NativeTube implements the same media-heavy desktop YouTube client three times in pure Rust—in `iced 0.14.0`, `GPUI` (`gpui-pre 0.3.12` + `gpui-component 0.9.1`), and `Rune` (`anomalyco/rune` rev `4fbd2d6`)—above one shared backend (`nt-core`: visionOS Innertube client, traffic gate, image cache, Chromium `ScrollAnimator` port, and `libmpv` software plus Wayland GPU subsurface playback) `[docs/blog/coordinator-notes.md: 9–15]`. Over twelve days, AI agents (Claude Opus 5.5, GPT 6.2 Sol, Gemini 4 Neon) built, audited, and tuned all three apps under one coordinator `[docs/blog/coordinator-notes.md: 17–19; docs/media/README.md: 9–13]`. Holding core logic constant exposes what each UI toolkit actually costs at runtime and in agent hours.

## 1. Verdict in 5 Bullets

- **Once video bypasses the UI tree, scrolling and memory—not playback—separate the frameworks.** Moving video to a desync Wayland `wl_subsurface` cut 720p30 playback CPU across all three apps from `41–42%` (`2caf0fa`) to `9.0–10.0%` (`NOW`, `32.72–35.24 M` instructions/s), leaving wheel-scroll CPU (`Rune 1,013.41 M` vs. `iced 1,540.33 M` vs. `GPUI 3,020.93 M` instructions) and baseline footprint (`iced 99 MiB` cold PSS / `29.6 MiB` binary vs. `GPUI 143 MiB` / `52.7 MiB` and `Rune 131 MiB` / `42.2 MiB`) as the decisive dividers `[docs/media/README.md: 47–52, 168, 196–209]`.
- **`iced 0.14` wins on runtime frugality and compile-time safety, paid for via a `1.57×` UI velocity tax.** It ships the smallest binary (`29.6 MiB`), fastest Home load (`209 ms`), lowest cold/warm/tour PSS (`99` / `153` / `267 MiB`), and **zero crashes** across `43` project incidents, but missing text ellipsis, blur, `unfocus()` helpers, and floating popups raised estimated YouTube-parity UI work to `~44 h` (`~$1,220`) `[docs/media/README.md: 50, 168–178; .cache/media/out/hiccups.json: 43; docs/review/youtube-parity.md: 117]`.
- **`GPUI` is the highest-leverage toolkit for AI agents building layered chrome—at `2–3×` the scroll CPU.** Built-in `anchored()`/`deferred()` popups, shadows, animations, and tab-group focus cut parity UI effort to `~28 h` (`~$780`), yet per-frame element rebuilds and Taffy layout burn `1,112.34 M` instructions/s on wheel scroll, pull `811` lockfile crates, and turn synchronous entity re-entrancy into runtime panics `[docs/review/youtube-parity.md: 117; docs/media/README.md: 202; docs/review/frontends.md: 414; docs/upstream/gpui-reentrant-keystroke.md: 10]`.
- **`Rune` validates fine-grained live values (`Live`, `LiveText`, `Pixels`) for high-frequency state, but remains too pre-1.0 for low-friction shipping.** Updating scroll offsets and scrubbers without re-running views yielded the smallest codebase (`2,508` lines) and lowest scroll cost (`373.52 M` instructions/s), yet required `[patch.crates-io]` forks of `winit`/`wgpu-hal`, `libloading` lookups for `wl_display`, leaked `&'static str` app IDs, and workarounds for release-mode `assert!` panics on wrapped or truncated `LiveText` `[docs/review/frontends.md: 412; docs/media/README.md: 208; apps/rune/NOTES.md: 4–6, 224–226, 290; docs/upstream/rune-wrapped-livetext.md: 26–30]`.
- **Integration choices and benchmark traps outweighed raw toolkit overhead.** Overriding `iced`'s `HighPerformance` GPU default halved bare-shell PSS (`120 MB` → `50 MB`), pinning Mesa EGL cut `Rune` renderer init `5×` (`505 ms` → `99 ms`), and asymmetric prewarm defaults at `NOW` explain nearly all of `Rune`'s apparent `189 ms` vs. `iced`'s `95 ms` `Open→frame` gap `[docs/upstream/iced-power-preference.md: 30–31; .cache/orchestrate/native-tube/ledger.tsv: 42; docs/media/README.md: 50–52, 79]`.

## 2. The Evidence, by Theme

### Speed: Startup, First Frame, and Prewarm Traps
At `NOW` (AC, `balance_performance`), `iced` maps its window in `190 ms` and loads Home in `209 ms`, beating `Rune` (`238 ms` / `252 ms`) and `GPUI` (`293 ms` / `310 ms`) `[docs/media/README.md: 190–310 ms]`. Bare shells without `nt-core` expose a hidden trap: out of the box, `iced 0.14` mapped *slowest* (`460 ms` busy `[docs/blog/coordinator-notes.md: 460 ms]`; `339 ms` isolated `[docs/upstream/iced-power-preference.md: 339 ms]`) because `iced_wgpu` requests `HighPerformance` and probes GL alongside Vulkan, waking the NVIDIA dGPU. Setting `WGPU_POWER_PREF=low` and `WGPU_BACKEND=vulkan` dropped bare `iced` to `175 ms` (`199 ms` isolated `[docs/upstream/iced-power-preference.md: 199 ms]`), ahead of `GPUI` (`253 ms`) and `Rune` (`259 ms`) `[docs/blog/coordinator-notes.md: 175–259 ms]`.

First-video `Open→frame` at `NOW` lands at `95 ms` (`iced`), `118 ms` (`GPUI`), and `189 ms` (`Rune`) `[docs/media/README.md: 95–189 ms]`—all crushing YouTube in Helium (`4170 ms` cold, `3885 ms` warm `[docs/blog/coordinator-notes.md: 4170, 3885 ms]`). What surprised me is that `Rune`'s `189 ms` lag is a prewarm asymmetry, not slower rendering: at `NOW`, `iced` and `GPUI` prewarm both `libmpv` and the Wayland GPU surface after Home draws, whereas `Rune` prewarms only `Player` `[docs/media/README.md: 79]`. When `iced` deferred GPU attach in gate `compare-isDkA304`, its `click_first_frame_ms` jumped from `97.30 ms` to `209.80 ms` `[.cache/perf/out/compare-isDkA304/summary.md: 97.30, 209.80 ms]` (failing its `+75.00 ms` budget), then fell to `112.10 ms` `[.cache/perf/out/compare-F70v7wbg/summary.md: 112.10 ms]` once surface prewarm landed `[.cache/orchestrate/native-tube/ledger.tsv: 43–44]`. Because `Rune`'s `2caf0fa` baseline skipped `libmpv` prewarm (`273.20 ms` → `277.30 ms` `[.cache/perf/out/compare-isDkA304/summary.md: 273.20, 277.30 ms]`), it passed the relative gate while still paying `~99 ms` of on-demand EGL init `[.cache/orchestrate/native-tube/ledger.tsv: 99 ms]`. Once warmed, `Warm switch ms` converges to `36 ms` (`iced`), `43 ms` (`GPUI`), and `50 ms` (`Rune`), inside 3-run noise (`33–88 ms`) `[docs/media/README.md: 36–50, 33–88 ms]`.

### Memory and Binary Footprint
Stripped binaries at `NOW` scale with crate graphs: `iced` is `29.6 MiB` (`431` crates), `Rune` is `42.2 MiB` (`500` crates), and `GPUI` is `52.7 MiB` (`811` crates) `[docs/media/README.md: 29.6–52.7 MiB; docs/review/frontends.md: 431–811]`. Cold Home PSS (`NT_NO_PREWARM=1`, zero `libmpv` maps) isolates UI weight at `99 MiB` (`iced`), `131 MiB` (`Rune`), and `143 MiB` (`GPUI`); Warm Home PSS rises to `153 MiB`, `169 MiB`, and `188 MiB` `[docs/media/README.md: 99–188 MiB]`.

In software playback, standalone `ntctl play` uses `116 MiB` PSS, leaving UI residuals of `95 MiB` (`iced`, `55%` core share), `129 MiB` (`Rune`, `47%`), and `139 MiB` (`GPUI`, `45%`) `[docs/media/README.md: 95–139 MiB]`. On GPU playback and the full tour, `Rune` climbs highest: GPU playback PSS hits `217 MiB` (`iced`), `253 MiB` (`GPUI`), and `274 MiB` (`Rune`), while Tour peak PSS reaches `267 MiB`, `289 MiB`, and `337 MiB` `[docs/media/README.md: 217–337 MiB]`. Meanwhile, post-Watch `warmed_retained_pss_mib` fell vs. `2caf0fa` by `-68.45 MiB` (to `48.86 MiB`) in `GPUI` and `-53.37 MiB` (to `77.75 MiB`) in `Rune` `[.cache/perf/out/compare-isDkA304/summary.md: 48.86, 77.75 MiB]`.

### CPU and GPU Work (and Two Counter Paradoxes)
Idle Home is event-driven across all three: `10 s` retires `6.02 M` (`iced`), `5.97 M` (`GPUI`), and `5.98 M` (`Rune`) user instructions (`0.60 M/s`) `[docs/media/README.md: 5.97–6.02 M]`.

**Scrolling** exposes the sharpest CPU divide. Over the 27-tick `wheel` story, `Rune` retires `1,013.41 M` instructions (`373.52 M/s`), `iced` `1,540.33 M` (`568.05 M/s`, `1.52×` `Rune`), and `GPUI` `3,020.93 M` (`1,112.34 M/s`, `2.98×` `Rune`); Home reload shows `173.43 M` (`iced`) and `178.11 M` (`Rune`) vs. `252.05 M` (`GPUI`) `[docs/media/README.md: 173.43–3,020.93 M]`.

**Playback** shows dramatic convergence. Software 720p30 playback retires `6,525.11–6,574.66 M` instructions (`21.75–21.92 M`/frame, `44–45%` CPU, `80–82%` core share) `[docs/media/README.md: 44–45%, 6,525.11–6,574.66 M]`. Switching to the Wayland GPU subsurface cuts user instructions **18.5–20×** to `352.61 M` (`iced`), `352.92 M` (`GPUI`), and `327.54 M` (`Rune`) (`1.09–1.18 M`/frame, `9.5–10%` CPU), with identical GPU energy (`1.54–1.56 J`) `[docs/media/README.md: 327.54–352.92 M, 1.54–1.56 J]`.

Two conflicting counter results stand out:
1. **Negative instruction residuals vs. higher cycle/CPU cost.** In software playback, `ntctl` retired *more* user instructions (`7,065.18 M`) than any GUI app (`6,525.11–6,574.66 M`), yielding negative residuals (`-539.9 M` `GPUI`, `-515.9 M` `iced`, `-487.6 M` `Rune` `[.cache/media/out/counters/counters.json: -539.9 to -487.6 M]`), yet `ntctl` used *fewer* cycles (`3,741.26 M` vs. `4,591.19–4,695.03 M`) and less CPU (`36%` vs. `44–45%`) `[docs/media/README.md: 3,741.26–7,065.18 M, 36%]`. `ntctl` omits `set_target_size`, scaling full `1280×720` `[.cache/media/out/counters/core/playback-software/1/ntctl.log: 1280x720]` at `1.89` IPC, whereas the two-column Watch page scales smaller (fewer SIMD instructions) while stalling memory bandwidth (`~1.41` IPC) on `~3.7 MB`/frame copies and wgpu uploads `[apps/iced/NOTES.md: ~3.7 MB]`.
2. **Contaminated system-wide wheel GPU/RAPL counters.** `iced | wheel` reports `48.12 J` package energy, `123.57 Mcycles` RCS, and `14.27 Mcycles` on the **VCS video decoder**, vs. `0.00` VCS and `19.29–20.22 J` for `GPUI`/`Rune` `[docs/media/README.md: 14.27–123.57 Mcycles, 19.29–48.12 J]`. Yet `iced`'s receipts show zero rendered video frames and elevated host load (`4.61` vs. `2.76` on `GPUI` `[.cache/media/out/counters/iced/wheel/1/sample.json: 4.61; .cache/media/out/counters/gpui/wheel/1/sample.json: 2.76]`). Because `i915`/RAPL counters are system-wide `[docs/media/README.md: 129]` and `counters.py` runs 3 slices back-to-back per app, concurrent desktop video activity polluted `iced`'s wheel GPU/energy rows.

### Stability
`stability.py` (20 related switches + Back, 3 runs/milestone) scored `[docs/media/README.md: 100]` (`w=0`) across all `30` assessable historical cells and `0/9` switch frame checks per app at `NOW` `[docs/media/README.md: 89–95, 244–248]` (`GPUI` was `n/a` at `96df4ec`/`cb25193` because transitive `serde_json/preserve_order` altered its fixture keys `[docs/media/README.md: 65]`).

Because historical runs used software rendering `[docs/media/README.md: 95]`, `.cache/media/out/hiccups.json` holds the ground truth: `[.cache/media/out/hiccups.json: 43]` incidents (all `43` fixed)—`14` in `nt-core`, `7` in `iced`, `7` in `Rune`, `6` in `GPUI`, `6` in orchestration (`5` model usage-cap pauses, `1` git-author rewrite), and `3` in test tooling. Notably, **`iced` had 0 crashes and 0 stalls**. **`GPUI` had 1 production crash** (`#10`: entity double-lease panic when synthetic cursor-hide keystrokes ran inside `cx.defer_in` `[docs/upstream/gpui-reentrant-keystroke.md: 9–16]`). **`Rune` had 1 release crash** (`#12`: wrapped `LiveText` tripping `assert!` at `flatten.rs:258` `[docs/upstream/rune-wrapped-livetext.md: 3–6]`) and **1 GPU switch stall** (`#13`: `3/5` timeouts when GPU frames didn't wake `Rune` to open its hole, plus `2.6 s` black holes `#14` `[apps/rune/NOTES.md: 236–242]`).

### Developer and Agent Experience
First-build Opus costs barely differed: `$23.54` / `49 min` (`iced`), `$27.99` / `55 min` (`Rune`), and `$28.41` / `59 min` (`GPUI`) `[docs/blog/coordinator-notes.md: $23.54–$28.41]`. Parity features (menus, miniplayer, chapters, heatmaps, storyboards, guide) split them wide: `GPUI` needed `~28 h` (`~$780`) of UI work versus `~40 h` (`~$1,100`) for `Rune` and `~44 h` (`~$1,220`) for `iced`, atop `~36 h` shared core work `[docs/review/youtube-parity.md: 28–44 h]`. At review, `Rune` totaled `2,508` lines, `GPUI` `3,045` (`+30`-line patch), and `iced` `3,762` `[docs/review/frontends.md: 2508–3762]`. `GPUI` drew the heatmap in `20` lines and the video hole in `~50` lines `[apps/gpui/NOTES.md: 20, ~50]`, whereas `iced` needed custom binary-search `Clamp` ellipsis, `Scroller` virtualization, and `1×1` image badges to dodge `iced_wgpu`'s quad-below-image layer order `[apps/iced/NOTES.md: 129–134]`.

### Framework Maturity
Agents filed `7` upstream repros `[docs/upstream/*.md]`:
- **`iced 0.14` (3):** `HighPerformance` GPU default and missing ellipsis (both fixed on `master` `98bd48fe` `[docs/upstream/iced-power-preference.md: 44–49, 82–85]`), zero-width `row!` children resetting sibling focus `[docs/upstream/iced-focus-sibling-width.md: 31–37]`, and missing `unfocus()` helper `[docs/upstream/iced-unfocus-helper.md: 3–8]`. Zero crate patches required.
- **`GPUI` `0.3.12` (3):** Linux lacks `paint_surface` `[apps/gpui/NOTES.md: 279–280]`, Wayland drops `wl_pointer.axis_stop` (vendored `gpui-pre-linux` fork `[docs/upstream/gpui-axis-stop.md: 32–40]`), `hide_cursor_until_mouse_moves` is private `[docs/upstream/gpui-hide-cursor.md: 33–38]`, and `dispatch_keystroke` inside updates panics `[docs/upstream/gpui-reentrant-keystroke.md: 43–55]`. On macOS, GPUI initially showed no text until static Roboto weights were bundled (`iced` showed dots; `Rune` worked) `[docs/blog/coordinator-notes.md: 125–127]`.
- **`Rune` `0.0.1` git (1 + pins):** Unpublished, requires workspace `[patch.crates-io]` forks of `winit`/`wgpu-hal` `[apps/rune/Cargo.toml: 24–26]`, `&'static str` app IDs `[apps/rune/NOTES.md: 290]`, `libloading` `dlopen` for `wl_proxy_get_display` `[apps/rune/NOTES.md: 224–226]`, and avoids `.wrap()`/`.truncate()` on `LiveText` to prevent release aborts `[docs/upstream/rune-wrapped-livetext.md: 26–30]`.

## 3. Why the Numbers Look Like That: Mechanisms

1. **Invalidation granularity drives scroll CPU.** `GPUI` rebuilds visible elements and runs Taffy flexbox every scroll frame (`0.4–0.7 ms` build + `2–3 ms` Taffy `[docs/review/frontends.md: 168–169]`), even with `TopBar`/`Rail` cached `[apps/gpui/NOTES.md: 178–185]`. `iced`'s `Scroller` shifts laid-out children via `with_translation(0, -offset)` with zero `view()` passes inside `0.5`-viewport bands `[apps/iced/NOTES.md: 61–75]` (autopilot `Scroll` messages force extra rebuilds real wheel input skips `[docs/review/frontends.md: 78–85]`). `Rune` writes `[0, -offset]` into `Live<[f32; 2]>` via `Tx::live_only()`, skipping view and layout completely between bands `[apps/rune/NOTES.md: 53–68]`.
2. **Subsurface offloading erases video differences.** Following `yt-music-gpui` `[docs/review/yt-music-gpui-notes.md: 16–34]`, rendering `mpv` OpenGL/VA-API into a desync child `wl_subsurface` (`wp_viewporter` clipped via `VideoSurface::set_clip` `[apps/iced/NOTES.md: 315–318]`) eliminated CPU RGBA buffers, `3.7 MB`/frame uploads, and 30 Hz UI redraws, converging all three to `9.5–10%` CPU.
3. **Compile-time trees vs. runtime leases govern crashes.** `iced` serializes mutations through `&mut App` in `update(Message)`, letting `rustc` forbid re-entrancy (`0` crashes). `GPUI`'s `EntityMap` leases and `Rune`'s `flatten.rs` commit assertions defer structural invariants to runtime panics that AI agents hit until stress-tested `[docs/upstream/gpui-reentrant-keystroke.md: 52–54; docs/upstream/rune-wrapped-livetext.md: 19–22]`.
4. **Driver mappings and dual caches dominate RAM.** `35–55 MiB` jumps stem from `cosmic-text` system font mmaps (`~34 MB` in `iced` `[apps/iced/NOTES.md: 147]`), `nt-core`'s `96 MiB` thumbnail cache `[apps/rune/NOTES.md: 304–305]`, CPU+GPU image copies in `GPUI`/`Rune` (`11–46 MiB` `[apps/gpui/NOTES.md: 111; apps/rune/NOTES.md: 306]`), and dual EGL vendor probing cured by `select_egl_vendor()` (`505 ms` → `99 ms` attach in `Rune` `[.cache/orchestrate/native-tube/ledger.tsv: 42]`).

## 4. Recommendations: Who Should Pick Which

- **Pick `GPUI` for chrome-rich workspaces built fast by AI agents or product teams.** Menus, popovers, animations, vector paths, and focus groups take ~one-third less effort (`~28 h` vs. `~44 h` `[docs/review/youtube-parity.md: 117]`), if you accept `~53 MiB` binaries and higher scroll CPU, apply `Entity::cached` early, ban `dispatch_*` inside `cx.defer_in`, and tolerate small Linux patches.
- **Pick `iced` for lean binaries, low CPU/RAM, crates.io stability, and crash-free state machines.** Best for broad Linux shipping without `[patch.crates-io]` forks (`99 MiB` cold PSS, `29.6 MiB` binary, `0` crashes `[docs/media/README.md: 50, 168]`). Budget `~1.5×` more UI effort for overlays and focus plumbing, and override `HighPerformance` wgpu defaults until `0.15` `[docs/upstream/iced-power-preference.md: 44–49]`.
- **Pick `Rune` only for live-telemetry or custom-physics surfaces where you own git pins—otherwise wait.** Its `Live`/`Pixels` design leads scroll efficiency (`373.52 M/s` in `2,508` lines `[docs/media/README.md: 208; docs/review/frontends.md: 412]`) and worked immediately on macOS Metal `[docs/blog/coordinator-notes.md: 127]`, but `0.0.1` git forks and `LiveText` assertions make general use premature.
- **Universal rule:** Put networking, caching, and scroll physics in a headless `core`, deny `unwrap`/`expect`/`panic` `[.cache/orchestrate/native-tube/ledger.tsv: 45]`, and render video on a compositor subsurface `[docs/review/yt-music-gpui-notes.md: 12–34]`.

## 5. Caveats and Open Questions

- **Split power regimes:** Timing/counter passes ran on AC (`balance_performance`), whereas all `39` `resources.py` runs used battery (`power`); an earlier power-saver pass ran `~50%` slower `[docs/media/README.md: 28–30]`.
- **System-wide counters and `n = 3` medians:** `i915`/RAPL counters include host activity `[docs/media/README.md: 129]` (polluting `iced | wheel` VCS/energy), 3-run medians leave `Warm switch` noisy (`33–88 ms` `[docs/media/README.md: 59]`), and per-PMU `cpu_core`/`cpu_atom` medians in `counters.json` don't sum to total instruction medians under thread migration (`9.74 M + 87.88 M != 173.43 M` `[.cache/media/out/counters/counters.json: 16–33]`).
- **Single-machine Wayland scope:** All runs used `1280×800` (`1x`) on one Arrow Lake-P + NVIDIA Hyprland laptop `[docs/media/README.md: 24, 129]`; HiDPI scaling, 4K VP9/AV1 `[docs/review/youtube-parity.md: 57]`, X11, Windows DXGI, and macOS Metal (`GPUI` `paint_surface`) remain unmeasured `[docs/macos.md: 3–5]`.
- **Pending input checks & ToS:** A final scheduled batch of physical chord/menu checks (`Shift+N`, `?`, `iced` menu sleep-timer) remained queued `[.cache/orchestrate/native-tube/ledger.tsv: 48]`. NativeTube is an engineering benchmark explicitly outside YouTube's Terms of Service `[docs/blog/coordinator-notes.md: 207–208]`.
