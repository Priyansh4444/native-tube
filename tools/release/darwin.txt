Unzip the download. It contains one app. Open that app.

The first time you open a copy downloaded from the project, macOS may say it
cannot be checked. Verify the SHA256, then Control-click the app and choose
Open. The app is ad hoc signed and is not notarized.

Playback libraries are inside the app. You do not need Homebrew or a separate
mpv install. The app needs an Apple silicon Mac running macOS 15 or newer.
BUILD.json, next to this file inside the app, records the linked system
libraries and the executable's Mach-O deployment target.
